# About RootMap MVP

RootMap MVP is a parent-facing Montessori companion designed to help families stay close to their child’s learning journey. It gives parents a clear, calm way to understand what their child is exploring, what they are learning, and which moments deserve attention at home.

At its core, RootMap is a simple idea: when parents can see what a child is interested in, how they repeat work, and how they grow over time, they can support that learning more intentionally. This prototype turns that idea into a practical digital experience for family connection and early childhood reflection.

## What RootMap focuses on

- Child-centered learning snapshots and progress tracking
- Montessori-inspired activities families can revisit at home
- Weekly planning for meaningful practice and routine
- Reflection on skills, independence, and repetition
- A family-friendly interface that feels warm and easy to use

## Who it is for

RootMap is designed for parents, caregivers, and family members who want a simple way to stay connected to their child’s Montessori experience without being overwhelmed by paperwork or fragmented updates.

## Design intent

The product is built to feel reassuring and structured. It gives parents quick access to their child’s learning story, helping them celebrate milestones, understand classroom experiences, and stay involved in daily growth.

## Product direction

This MVP explores the essential experience of a parent companion:

- each child has a personal profile and learning journey
- activities are easy to discover and revisit
- weekly plans can feel intentional and connected to the child’s interests
- observations become a simple record of meaningful moments and growth

RootMap MVP is a prototype for product exploration and family-centered design feedback. It is not yet a production-grade system, but it is a strong foundation for a more complete solution that supports parents in everyday early learning.
